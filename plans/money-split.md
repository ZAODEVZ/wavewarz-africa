# Money split and artist wallets

- **State:** waiting on a pick
- **Owner:** IMan (recommendation), Zaal (decision)
- **From:** iman-desk [#78](https://github.com/ZAODEVZ/iman-desk/issues/78)

## The question

Zaal's words: "do 50% to wavewarz africa and 50% to each artist or just keep all to treasury".

## The recommendation (IMan, 13 Oct)

**50% to the WaveWarZ Africa wallet and 50% to the artist, on every battle. Each artist battles from their own Solana wallet.**

- When every battle carries the one shared wallet on the artist side, on chain each battle reads as one artist against themselves, and nobody can be ranked.
- Keeping everything in the treasury leaves that problem as it is.
- An artist with no wallet yet battles as "unranked" until they have one. No battle waits on it.

## Wallets

Each artist makes a free Solana wallet on their own phone and shares only the public address. The list of names and addresses is kept off this public repo.
